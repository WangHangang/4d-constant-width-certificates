## Raised-body upper cubature

Use v=(1,a,a*s)/sqrt(D), D=1+a^2+a^2*s^2, and q=tan(phi/2).
The spherical factors are d sigma_2 = a D^(-3/2) da ds and
sin(phi)^2 d phi = 8 q^2/(1+q^2)^3 dq.
Including the radial-volume factor 1/4 gives kappa=48 for 24 chambers
and kappa=12 for six chambers. A certified ball-centre constraint
supplies a pointwise upper bound for the radial function throughout a cell.
Gauss nodes, weights, and the fourth-derivative remainder are enclosed.

The compact checker verifies domain coverage and every leaf, rather than
accepting old per-cell upper estimates or floating global totals.
Its elementary interval operations use binary64 and immediate outward
nextafter expansion; this is part of the arithmetic contract, not exact
real arithmetic or a formal-proof-assistant verification.
